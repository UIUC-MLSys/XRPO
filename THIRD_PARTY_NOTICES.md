## Datasets

The training mixture and the ICL corpus derive from public datasets, which keep
their own licenses:

| Dataset | Source |
| --- | --- |
| DAPO-Math-17k | https://huggingface.co/datasets/BytedTsinghua-SIA/DAPO-Math-17k |
| DeepCoder-Preview-Dataset (primeintellect / taco / lcbv5 / codeforces) | https://huggingface.co/datasets/agentica-org/DeepCoder-Preview-Dataset |

`icl_corpus/*_similar_questions.json` contains the verbatim question text of
those datasets (as nearest-neighbour maps). `icl_corpus/*_icl_corpus_*.json`
contains solutions generated by Qwen models and is therefore also subject to
the corresponding Qwen model licenses.
